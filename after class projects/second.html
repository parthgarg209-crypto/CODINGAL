Dr. A.P.J Abdul Kalam

Introduction

Dr. A.P.J Abdul Kalamwas a renowed Indian scientist and former President of India. He was also known as the Missile Man of India.because of his contribution towards the development of India's missile technology.

Birth

Dr. A.P.J Abdul Kalam was born on October 15, 1931, in a small town of Tamil Nadu in Rameswaram

Achievements

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam developed India's

SLV-III rocket
and contributed to the development of
Agni
and
Prithvi
missiles. He played an important role in the Pokhran-II nuclear tests and received the Bharat Ratna in 1997. He also served as the 11th President of India.
